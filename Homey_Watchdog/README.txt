Homey Watchdog combines a transparent health overview of Flows, devices, apps and dependencies with scheduled battery-device monitoring.

The health scan provides category scores and prioritized findings. The battery watchdog checks Homey's existing device timestamps at a configurable interval, warns when battery-capable devices stop reporting, and can deliver notifications to Homey Timeline and all Homey users by mobile push.

All analysis and state storage run locally on Homey Pro. The app does not control devices or repair Flows automatically. Device exclusions, notification settings and a JSON report export are available in the settings dashboard.
