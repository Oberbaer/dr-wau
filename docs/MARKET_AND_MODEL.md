# Market and business model

Snapshot: 2026-08-12.

Homey describes its ecosystem as hundreds of thousands of users, concentrated primarily in Europe. This is a platform-wide figure, not a count of German Homey Pro owners with complex installations or buying intent. The realistically addressable service segment is therefore much smaller.

The product should be treated as a trust and lead-generation tool:

1. A Homey owner installs the free app and receives real value without registration.
2. The owner exports a privacy-safe report.
3. The owner may independently ask a certified installer to review or repair the prioritized items.
4. The installer works only after a defined scope, price and owner-approved access.
5. A second scan documents the before/after result.

Example scenario, not a forecast: 2,000 installs × 5% qualified help requests × 20% conversion equals 20 paid jobs. At an average service ticket of EUR 250 this would be EUR 5,000 revenue. The important variables are Store reach, trust, geographic coverage, conversion and time per job—not the theoretical number of Homey users.

Recommended services:

- Remote report review with a fixed price.
- Scoped Flow/device repair with backup and verification.
- Full installation health check with before/after report.
- Optional maintenance retainer for professional customers.

Do not promise automatic optimization or guaranteed energy savings. Score improvements must represent resolved evidence, not sales pressure.
