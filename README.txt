Dr. Wau combines a transparent health overview of Flows, devices, apps and dependencies with scheduled battery-device monitoring and configuration backups.

The health scan provides category scores and prioritized findings. The battery watchdog checks Homey's existing device timestamps at a configurable interval, warns when battery-capable devices stop reporting, and can deliver notifications to Homey Timeline and all Homey users by mobile push.

Health analysis runs locally on Homey Pro and does not control devices. Entire zones, including their subzones, can be excluded from battery alerts and device findings. Backups can be downloaded or sent to destinations configured by the owner. Selective restore requires an explicit choice and confirmation.
